# NOCTURNE 4.1.1

- The home hero now has built-in motion immediately after installation: drifting crimson/charcoal light, a slowly rotating gothic sigil, and a subtle entrance animation.
- The animation is bundled in app CSS and does not require an uploaded MP4/GIF or a network connection.
- The Appearance preview now displays the bundled default artwork with its motion overlay.
- Custom MP4/GIF uploads remain optional and can replace the built-in banner.
