# Attribution and third-party services

NOCTURNE is an independent client and is not affiliated with OtakuDesu,
MyAnimeList, AniList, MiteNime, AniMap, or Anime News Network.

- Anime metadata, posters, ratings and related records remain the property of their respective providers and rightsholders.
- Tenrai is a third-party MyAnimeList-compatible catalogue provider; it is not an official MyAnimeList API.
- AniList, MiteNime, AniMap and Anime News Network are external providers. Their uptime, terms, schemas and rate limits may change.
- Video embeds are sourced from public pages being viewed. NOCTURNE does not download video streams, remove DRM, or bypass server-side embedding/security restrictions.
- Chaquopy is used to embed the Python runtime in the Android app. Flask, Requests, Beautiful Soup and urllib3 retain their respective licenses.

Review each provider's current terms before redistributing or operating the app at scale.
